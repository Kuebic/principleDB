Grace precedes obedience. We are loved first, and our obedience flows out of
being loved — not the other way around. The person who tries to earn love has
mistaken the order of things.

The empty seat is the assignment. God seats the person who is not yet here next
to the people who are, so that the room is never only for those already in it.

Mercy is not the suspension of justice but its deepest fulfilment. To forgive is
not to pretend the wound did not happen; it is to refuse to let the wound have
the last word.

A small faithfulness, repeated, outweighs a large intention never acted on. The
character we become is the sum of the ordinary choices no one applauds.
